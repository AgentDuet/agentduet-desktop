Install it

Right now this is running from wherever you downloaded it. Installing puts it somewhere permanent and adds it to your applications menu.

This matters beyond tidiness: your AI assistant is told where to launch the secretary from. Move the file afterwards and that link quietly breaks.

Your key is checked against the provider before it is saved, so a wrong paste fails here rather than silently later. It is stored only on this machine.

Your B3 connector

This gives it a phone number and a message channel — how people reach you. Skip it if you do not have one yet; everything else works without it, and you can add it later in settings.

Your AI assistant

You drive the secretary from an assistant you already use — ask it what is waiting, tell it what you do, have it reply for you. There is no dashboard; this is it.
