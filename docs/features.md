# AgentDuet Desktop: features

A short list to share. Keep it in step with what ships: add a line when a feature lands, take one
out when it goes.

**Calls**
- Records both sides of every call on your line, incoming and outgoing.
- Works with your mobile number or a SIP phone, and calls ring through as normal.
- One small audio file per call (`.m4a`), saved in a folder you choose.
- Stays on your Mac: no copy with B3, and kept out of iCloud unless you turn that on.
- Callers shown by name from your Mac's Contacts.

**Transcripts**
- Every call transcribed on your Mac, with no cloud.
- Handles mixed languages, such as English, Malay, Mandarin, Vietnamese and Thai.
- Live captions during the call, marked "Live", then "Draft" until the final transcript is ready.
- Fix a transcript by hand, and teach it words to listen for, up to 10.

**The assistant (on your Mac)**
- Ask about your calls: "What did the supplier say about the price?"
- Searches all your calls by meaning, not just exact words.
- Summarises and translates transcripts.
- Builds a short profile of each person from your calls.
- Drafts a calendar event or email for you to send.

**Everyday**
- Sign in with Google, and nothing to configure.
- A menu bar icon that keeps running when the window is closed, and can start at login.
- Right-click a call to delete its recording and transcript.
- Tells you when a new version is out.

**Editions:** AgentDuet Recorder is the Calls section only, with no AI. AgentDuet AI adds the
transcripts and assistant to a Recorder.
